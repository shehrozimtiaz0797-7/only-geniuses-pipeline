Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Only Geniuses Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Only Geniuses YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

What Google data the app uses

The app asks for permission to upload videos to YouTube and to read the name of the signed-in channel (so the owner can confirm the right channel is connected). It uses this permission only to upload Only Geniuses quiz videos to the owner's own channel.

What the app stores

The app keeps one login token, stored privately by the channel owner, so videos can be uploaded on a schedule. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not access anyone else's account, videos, comments or viewer data.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Only Geniuses YouTube channel.
